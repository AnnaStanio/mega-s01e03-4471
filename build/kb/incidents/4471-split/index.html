The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. It reaches us through every channel and every tenant. Tracked in issue 1.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads the date day first. Only booking-v3 tenants, only through the form. The swap happens before the parser, so the parser fix does not touch it. Tracked in issue 2.

one-day: bookings move by one day. Day and month stay put; the date is a day early or late. Lisbon sees Tuesday and Toronto sees Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before its booking. That points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk had read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but still counted them under the parser. Tracked in issue 3.

Why it was one ticket

  • The dedupe rule merges on the subject line. All three problems arrive under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so the rule cannot tell them apart. Only the body and the tenant can.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. It calls form-hint reports parser and parser reports hint. Place a report by its tenant, its form and its channel.

What changes on the desk

  • #4471 is not “known”. Keep it for parser only; move the other reports to their tickets.
  • A new date report: read the body. Two dates with day and month swapped through a booking-v3 form is hint; any other swap is parser; a date a day off is one-day.
