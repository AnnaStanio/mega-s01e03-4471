The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April goes out as 4 July. It reaches us through every channel and every tenant. Fixed (“fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales”); issue 1 is closed.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY, people follow it, and the form reads the date day first. Only booking-v3 tenants, only through the form. The swap happens before the parser, so the parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so the hint is gone with it; issue 2 is closed as not planned.

one-day: bookings move by one day. Day and month stay put; the date is a day early or late. Lisbon sees Tuesday and Toronto sees Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before its booking. That points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk had read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but still counted them under the parser. Open: neither the parser fix nor the form’s retirement touches it. Tracked in issue 3.

Why it was one ticket

  • The dedupe rule merges on the subject line. All three problems arrive under the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Dates mixed up”), so the rule cannot tell them apart. Only the body and the tenant can.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. It calls form-hint reports parser and parser reports hint. Place a report by its tenant, its form and its channel.

What changes on the desk

  • #4471 was not one known issue. Closing it closed two problems, not three.
  • A new date report: read the body. A date a day off is one-day: add it to issue 3. A new day/month swap after the parser fix is a regression: open a new ticket, do not reopen #4471.
